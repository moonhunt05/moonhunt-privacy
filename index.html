Moonhunt Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026

EnglishDeutsch

Who we are

Moonhunt is a game by Hamed Stita, Moosacher Str. 81, 80809 Munich, Germany ("we"). For any question about your data, or to see or delete it, write to moonhunt05@gmail.com.

The short version

  • There's no sign-up: no email, phone number, real name or password.
  • Ads only when you ask for one: WATCH AD on a chest plays a short video from Google AdMob. Google's ad software in the game gets some data for that (see "Ads" below); in the EU, the UK and Switzerland it asks for your consent first. Apart from that, no outside tracking or analytics companies: what we count about how the game is used stays with us. We never sell your data.
  • Payments go through Google Play. We never see your card or bank details.

Delete your data

To have your Moonhunt account and its data deleted:

  1. In the game, open SETTINGS (the gear at the top right of the menu) and note your Player ID.
  2. Email moonhunt05@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my data" and your Player ID. If the game isn't on your phone any more, give us your player name and about how many trophies you had, and we'll look for your account.
  3. We delete it within 30 days and tell you when it's done.

What's deleted: your game account (player number, player name, progress, collection and trophies), your match records, device details, feedback, the statistics of your menu use, your ads count and any save code. What's kept: the records of your purchases, for as long as tax law requires (in Germany, up to 10 years). Deleted data also leaves our backups within 30 days. A copy of your account in your Google Play Games saved games (Android) is in your own Google account: you delete it in Play Games' settings. Deleting can't be undone, and what was bought for the account is lost with it.

What the game keeps, and why

  1. Your game account. When you first start the game, it makes a random player number and a secret code and keeps them on your phone. Our server keeps the player number, a scrambled (hashed) copy of the code, and your progress: your player name (made from the game's word lists, never your real name), your skins, creatures, coins, gems, chests, trophies, Moon Pass, quests and Trophy Road. Why: so you keep your progress and can play online.
  2. Matches. For every online match: both player numbers, the map, the result, how long it lasted and how it ended. Why: to find you rivals, for trophies and the leaderboard (other players see your player name and trophies there), and to keep the game fair and balanced.
  3. Device details. The name of your phone's system (for example "Android") and the game's version. Why: to know which versions are still played.
  4. Feedback. Stars, ticked topics and a comment, only when you send them from the feedback screen. Why: to make the game better. Please don't write personal details in the comment.
  5. Purchases. Google Play takes the payment, then gives the game a receipt (order number, product, purchase code and time), which the game sends to our server. The server checks it and keeps a record. Why: to give you what you bought, to make sure each purchase counts only once, and for our accounts. With each purchase the game also gives Google a scrambled tag made from your player number, which helps Google spot fraud. If a purchase is refunded or charged back, Google Play tells our server, which takes back what the purchase gave. Google's privacy policy applies to the payment itself: policies.google.com/privacy.
  6. How the menus are used. Which screens you open, which shop offers you tap, whether a purchase was finished or cancelled, and how far you get in HOW TO PLAY, with your player number. Why: to see what works and what to make better.
  7. Save codes. When you ask for a save code (to move your progress to another phone), the server keeps a scrambled copy of it until it's used, at most 24 hours. Why: so only you can move your account.
  8. Google Play Games (Android). If you're signed in to Google Play Games, the game keeps a copy of your player number and secret code (with your player name, skin, creature and trophies) in your Play Games saved games, which Google stores in your Google account. Why: so a reinstalled game, or a new phone with the same Google account, gets your progress back by itself. The game doesn't ask Google for your Play Games name, picture or friends. You can delete the saved game in Google Play Games' settings. Google's privacy policy applies to Play Games: policies.google.com/privacy.
  9. Emails to us. If you write to us (SUPPORT in the game's settings), we use your email address and what you write only to answer you. Why: to help you.
  10. Ads you watch. How many ads you watched today (at most 6 a day) and which chest they sped up, with your player number. Why: to open your chest sooner, and to keep to the daily limit.
  11. Internet address. Your phone's IP address is needed to connect to our server while you play. The game server doesn't write it down.

Android's own backup may also keep the game's settings and player number in your Google account, so a new phone can bring your progress back. You switch that backup on or off in your phone's settings.

Ads (Google AdMob)

The game shows rewarded ads, only when you tap WATCH AD on a chest (it then opens sooner). They come from Google AdMob (Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland). To load and show ads, count them and keep out fraud, Google's ad software in the game sends Google, whenever it loads an ad (also before you tap WATCH AD, so that one is ready): your phone's advertising ID, its IP address (which tells roughly where you are), information about the phone and the app, and how you use the ads (for example which ad was shown and whether it was watched to the end). We don't get this data. Google uses it under its own responsibility: see how Google uses information from apps that use its services and Google's privacy policy.

In the EU, the UK and Switzerland, Google's consent form asks you at the first start whether ads may be personalized and whether information may be stored on and read from your phone for ads. Without your consent you only get ads that don't use such information ("limited ads"), or none. You can change your answer at any time in the game's SETTINGS, under AD CONSENT. Everywhere, you can reset or delete your phone's advertising ID in the phone's own settings (on most Android phones: Settings > Google > Ads).

Google may also process this data in the USA. Google LLC is certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework.

Legal grounds (GDPR)

Running the game, your account and your purchases: Art. 6(1)(b) (our agreement with you). Match statistics, fairness and security: Art. 6(1)(f) (our legitimate interest in a game that works and is fair). Keeping purchase records: Art. 6(1)(c) (tax law). Counting the ads you watched: Art. 6(1)(b). Ads that are personalized or use information stored on your phone: your consent, Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR and § 25(1) TDDDG (the German law on storing and reading information on devices); you can withdraw it at any time (SETTINGS > AD CONSENT).

Where it's kept, and who helps us

Our server is rented from DigitalOcean and stands in Frankfurt, Germany; DigitalOcean stores the data for us under a data processing agreement. Payments are handled by Google, and the ads come from Google (see "Ads"). Nobody else gets your data from us.

How we protect it

Only we can log in to the server, and a firewall closes everything the game doesn't need. The game's connection to the server is not encrypted, so the game never sends anything sensitive through it: no real names, emails, passwords or payment details. Backups are made every day and kept for 30 days.

How long we keep it

Your game account, match records, device details and feedback are kept for as long as Moonhunt runs, so you don't lose your progress, unless you ask us to delete them. Purchase records are kept for as long as tax law requires (in Germany, up to 10 years). Deleted data also leaves the backups within 30 days.

Your rights

You can ask to see your data, get a copy, have it corrected or deleted, or have its use restricted, and you can object to its use. Write to moonhunt05@gmail.com with your player ID (in the game's SETTINGS), so we can find your account. Deleting an account can't be undone, and what was bought for it is lost with it. You also have the right to complain to a data protection authority.

Uninstalling the game deletes the player number and code from your phone; the account on our server stays until you ask us to delete it, and a save code, Google Play Games or Android's backup can bring it back to a phone.

Children

Moonhunt is not made for children under 13, and we don't knowingly keep data about them. If you think a child has played, write to us and we'll delete the account.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be on this page with a new date.

Das Spiel zeigt Belohnungswerbung, nur wenn du an einer Truhe auf WATCH AD tippst (dann geht sie früher auf). In der EU, im Vereinigten Königreich und in der Schweiz fragt dich Googles Einwilligungsfenster beim ersten Start, ob Werbung personalisiert werden darf und ob dafür Informationen auf deinem Handy gespeichert und ausgelesen werden dürfen. Wo die Daten liegen, und wer uns hilft

Unser Server ist bei DigitalOcean gemietet und steht in Frankfurt am Main; DigitalOcean speichert die Daten für uns im Rahmen eines Auftragsverarbeitungsvertrags. Zahlungen wickelt Google ab, und die Werbung kommt von Google (siehe „Werbung“). Sonst bekommt niemand deine Daten von uns.

Wie wir sie schützen

Nur wir können uns am Server anmelden, und eine Firewall sperrt alles, was das Spiel nicht braucht. Die Verbindung zwischen Spiel und Server ist nicht verschlüsselt, deshalb schickt das Spiel nichts Sensibles darüber: keine echten Namen, E-Mails, Passwörter oder Zahlungsdaten. Sicherungskopien werden täglich erstellt und 30 Tage aufbewahrt.

Wie lange wir sie speichern

Dein Spielkonto, Spielaufzeichnungen, Geräteangaben und Feedback bleiben gespeichert, solange es Moonhunt gibt, damit dein Fortschritt nicht verloren geht, außer du bittest uns, sie zu löschen. Kaufbelege bewahren wir so lange auf, wie das Steuerrecht es verlangt (in Deutschland bis zu 10 Jahre). Gelöschte Daten verschwinden innerhalb von 30 Tagen auch aus den Sicherungskopien.
